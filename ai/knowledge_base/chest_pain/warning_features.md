# Warning Features in Chest Pain

## Cardiac Red Flags
- Pain radiating to the left arm, shoulder, jaw, or back
- Pain associated with exertion or emotional stress
- Pain at rest lasting >20 minutes
- Associated diaphoresis (sweating)
- Associated nausea or vomiting
- Associated dyspnea (shortness of breath)
- Syncope or near-syncope
- Known coronary artery disease, prior MI, or heart failure
- Hypertension, diabetes, hyperlipidemia, smoking
- Family history of premature coronary artery disease

## Non-Cardiac Red Flags (to consider)
- Pain associated with swallowing (esophageal)
- Pain worsened by lying flat, improved by leaning forward (pericarditis)
- Pain with fever and productive cough (pneumonia)
- Sudden onset of severe pain (aortic dissection)
- Pain reproducible by palpation (musculoskeletal)

## Note
The presence of any red flag warrants immediate clinical assessment.
