# Chest Pain History Taking

## Essential Questions
- Onset: When did the chest pain start?
- Provocation: What makes it better or worse?
- Quality: How would you describe the pain? (e.g., pressure, burning, stabbing)
- Region: Where is the pain located? Does it radiate?
- Severity: On a scale of 0-10, how bad is the pain?
- Timing: Is it constant or intermittent? How long does each episode last?
- Associated symptoms: Do you have shortness of breath, sweating, nausea, dizziness, or palpitations?

## Associated Symptoms to Inquire
- Sweating (diaphoresis)
- Shortness of breath (dyspnea)
- Nausea or vomiting
- Dizziness or lightheadedness
- Palpitations
- Syncope or near-syncope

## Warning Features (Red Flags)
- Pain radiating to left arm, jaw, or back
- Associated with exertion
- Occurring at rest
- Associated with syncope
- Known heart disease
