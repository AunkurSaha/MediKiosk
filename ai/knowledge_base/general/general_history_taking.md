# General Clinical History Taking

## Systematic Symptom Assessment
- Onset: When did your symptoms begin, and did they start suddenly or gradually?
- Duration and Course: Has the condition been continuous, worsening, or episodic?
- Severity: How severely does this problem affect your daily activities on a scale of 0 to 10?
- Character and Quality: How does the sensation or discomfort feel?
- Aggravating and Relieving Factors: What activities, positions, or medicines make it better or worse?

## Review of Related Systems
- Respiratory: Any cough, shortness of breath, wheezing, or sputum?
- Cardiovascular: Any chest discomfort, palpitations, leg swelling, or dizziness?
- Gastrointestinal: Any nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, diarrhea, or appetite changes?
- Neurological: Any headache, weakness, numbness, or balance difficulties?
- Constitutional: Any fever, chills, night sweats, fatigue, or unexplained weight changes?

## Background Medical Profile
- Existing chronic conditions: Diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, kidney disease, lung disease
- Current medications: Prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines, herbal or home supplements
- Known allergies: Drug reactions, food allergies, environmental triggers
