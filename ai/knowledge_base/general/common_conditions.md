# Common Health Conditions and Symptom Inquiries

## Diabetes and Blood Sugar
- Diabetes Mellitus (type 1 or type 2)
- High blood sugar (hyperglycemia) or low blood sugar (hypoglycemia)
- Important questions: How is your blood sugar or diabetes currently managed, and have you checked your levels recently?
- Symptoms: excessive thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, blurred vision, numbness or tingling in feet
- Complications: diabetic ketoacidosis, neuropathy, nephropathy, retinopathy

## Hypertension and Blood Pressure
- High blood pressure (hypertension)
- Important questions: Do you have a history of high blood pressure, and are you currently taking any blood pressure medication?
- Symptoms: headache, dizziness, vision changes, chest tightness, palpitations
- Complications: cardiovascular disease, stroke, renal impairment

## Asthma and Respiratory Conditions
- Asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), bronchitis
- Important questions: Do you have a history of asthma or wheezing, or do you use an inhaler?
- Symptoms: shortness of breath, wheezing, persistent cough, chest tightness with breathing
- Triggers: allergens, cold air, physical exertion, respiratory infections

## Gastroesophageal Reflux and Digestive Symptoms
- Acid reflux, gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), gastritis, dyspepsia
- Important questions: Does the discomfort feel like burning or acid reflux, especially after meals or when lying down?
- Symptoms: burning in chest or upper abdomen, sour taste in mouth, difficulty swallowing, nausea
- Modifying factors: worse after eating or lying flat, relieved by antacids

## Headache and Neurological Symptoms
- Migraine, tension headache, cluster headache, secondary headache
- Important questions: Are you having any severe headache, vision changes, or numbness or tingling?
- Warning features: sudden onset thunderclap headache, focal neurological deficit, fever with neck stiffness
