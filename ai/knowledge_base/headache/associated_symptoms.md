# Associated Symptoms and Sensory Disturbance in Headache

## Visual Aura and Neurological Features
- Question intent: Differentiate migraine with aura from other headache types.
- Clinical domain: associated_symptoms
- Guidance: Transient reversible neurological disturbances such as visual scintillating scotoma, zigzag flashing lines, or unilateral tingling developing over 5-20 minutes before headache onset suggest migraine aura.
- Inquire: Did you notice any visual changes such as flashing lights, zigzag lines, or blind spots before the pain began?

## Autonomic and Gastrointestinal Symptoms
- Question intent: Assess nausea, vomiting, photophobia, and cranial autonomic signs.
- Clinical domain: associated_symptoms
- Guidance: Photophobia and phonophobia are classic migraine markers; severe nausea or vomiting impairs oral hydration; conjunctival injection, lacrimation, and nasal congestion occur in trigeminal autonomic cephalalgias.
- Inquire: Are you experiencing nausea, vomiting, or heightened sensitivity to light or sound?
