# Headache Red Flags and Emergency Warning Features

## Thunderclap Onset
- Question intent: Identify potential subarachnoid hemorrhage or acute intracranial catastrophe.
- Clinical domain: red_flags_neurological
- Guidance: Sudden explosive headache reaching maximal intensity within 1 minute ("worst headache of life") requires emergency evaluation regardless of prior headache history.
- Inquire: Did this headache hit like a clap of thunder or reach full severity within moments?

## Systemic and Neurological Signs
- Question intent: Detect meningitis, intracranial infection, or space-occupying lesions.
- Clinical domain: red_flags_neurological
- Guidance: Headache accompanied by fever, neck stiffness, confusion, altered mental status, seizures, or focal neurological deficits (unilateral weakness, numbness, speech difficulty) indicates urgent secondary headache etiology.
- Inquire: Are you experiencing any fever, stiff neck, confusion, weakness, or difficulty speaking?
